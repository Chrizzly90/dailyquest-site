[ DAILYQUEST ]

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

In short: DailyQuest stores all data only on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking. Nobody but you – not even the developer – has access to your data.

1. Controller

Christian Rink
Email: christian.rink@magenta.de

2. What data is processed

The app only processes data you enter yourself or allow it to access:

  • Your quests and settings: names, targets, units, penalty mode, reminder times, daily reset.
  • Your progress: values counted per day, history, XP, rank and streak.
  • Data from Apple Health (only with your permission, see section 3).

This data is kept in the app's local database on your device. It is not transferred to the developer or to third parties. Depending on your settings, it may be part of your iPhone backup with Apple (iCloud Backup) or on your computer.

3. Apple Health (health data)

When you tap “Connect”, iOS asks whether DailyQuest may read the following data:

  • Steps
  • Active energy
  • Workouts (start and duration)

Purpose: to track the steps and active energy quests automatically and to award bonus XP for workouts. For this, the app stores daily totals and workout minutes in its local database.

DailyQuest never writes data to Apple Health and never shares health data – neither with the developer nor with third parties, and in particular not for advertising or marketing.

You can revoke access at any time: Health app › Profile › Apps › DailyQuest. The app keeps working without access; the Health quests are then not counted.

4. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. There is no push server and no data transfer. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.

5. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

DailyQuest contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. The app does not need an internet connection.

Apple provides developers with aggregated statistics (such as downloads) and crash reports if you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings. Apple processes this data under its own responsibility; it does not reveal your training data.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Providing the app's features: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Health data from Apple Health: your explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, given via the iOS prompt and revocable at any time.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. Deleting DailyQuest from your iPhone removes all of the app's data. Data in Apple Health is not affected.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to withdraw consent with effect for the future. Because all data stays on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app or the Health app; the developer has no access to your data. For questions, contact the controller at the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

If the app's features change, this privacy policy will be updated. The version published here applies.

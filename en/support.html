FAQ

How do I connect Apple Health?

Tap “Connect” on the Steps or Active Energy tile. In the iOS prompt, turn on steps, active energy and workouts, scroll all the way down and tap “Allow”.

My steps stay at 0.

For privacy reasons, iOS doesn't tell apps whether read access was granted. Check access in the Health app under Profile › Apps › DailyQuest. The iPhone Simulator has no step data.

How much XP can I earn per day?

100 XP when all quests are done, plus 10 XP per day of your streak (up to +100 XP) and up to 50 XP workout bonus – a maximum of 250 XP per day. The workout bonus only counts on days with all quests done. See Settings › XP per day in the app.

What's the difference between Soft and Hard mode?

Soft: missed days cost XP and your streak. Hard: missed reps are added to the next day, and several missed days in a row cost you a rank. You can switch at any time; days already evaluated stay as they are.

When is a day evaluated?

At the daily reset (midnight by default, adjustable). If your iPhone is locked at that moment, the app evaluates the day the next time you unlock it or open the app.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting DailyQuest removes it completely. Data in Apple Health is not affected.

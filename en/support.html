FAQ

How do I connect Apple Health?

Tap “Connect” on the Steps or Active Energy tile. In the iOS prompt, turn on steps, active energy and workouts, scroll all the way down and tap “Allow”.

My steps stay at 0.

For privacy reasons, iOS doesn't tell apps whether read access was granted. Check access in the Health app under Profile › Apps › DailyQuest. The iPhone Simulator has no step data.

How much XP can I earn per day?

100 XP when all quests are done, plus 10 XP per day of your streak (up to +100 XP) and up to 50 XP workout bonus – a maximum of 250 XP per day, or 350 XP on a Red Gate day. The workout bonus only counts on days with all quests done. See Settings › XP per day in the app.

What's the difference between Soft, Hard and Red Gate?

Soft: missed days cost XP and your streak. Hard: missed reps are added to the next day, and several missed days in a row cost you a rank. Red Gate: like Hard – plus a red gate opens every 2–4 days (see below). You can switch at any time except on a Red Gate day; days already evaluated stay as they are.

What is a Red Gate?

In Red Gate mode a red gate opens at random every 2–4 days. On that day all targets are raised by ×1.1 to ×1.5 – steps and active energy included. Quests, targets, mode and daily reset can't be changed that day. Complete every quest for ×1.5 XP on your daily score. The next day your normal targets apply again.

What is sick mode?

When you're ill or injured, turn on sick mode in Settings, optionally with an end date. You can end it on the Today tab. While it's on, nothing is penalized: streak, XP and rank stay as they are, no penalty reps are added and there are no reminders. If you complete all quests anyway, the day counts as normal. “Reduced goals” lets you set lower targets. Only you turn the mode on – the app doesn't use health data to detect illness.

I forgot to turn on sick mode.

In History you can mark past days as sick afterwards (menu at the top right or in a day's details). Streak, XP, rank and penalty reps are then recalculated as if sick mode had been on for those days. Your counted values are kept, and you can undo the marking at any time.

When is a day evaluated?

At the daily reset (midnight by default, adjustable). If your iPhone is locked at that moment, the app evaluates the day the next time you unlock it or open the app.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting DailyQuest removes it completely. Data in Apple Health is not affected.
